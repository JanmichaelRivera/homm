01 WHY HOM EXISTS

Healthcare is a path.
Nobody should walk it alone.

HOM exists to make the healthcare journey feel more possible, more connected, and more accessible.

We bring students together with people, knowledge, experiences, and opportunities that can help turn curiosity into a career.

Whether someone is just exploring healthcare or already building their professional future, HOM is designed to be a community they can come back to.

Built around one simple belief: representation creates possibility.
